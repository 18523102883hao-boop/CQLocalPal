The "Flying Kiss"
Known as the most beautiful crack on Earth, this narrow, deep karst canyon features sheer cliffs, hanging waterfalls, clear streams, and lush greenery. A suspended wooden path takes you through a "line of sky" and into a quiet, primeval world. Cool, refreshing, and visually stunning — perfect for nature lovers and photographers. Your guide will drop you off at the entrance and pick you up at the exit for this stop.
