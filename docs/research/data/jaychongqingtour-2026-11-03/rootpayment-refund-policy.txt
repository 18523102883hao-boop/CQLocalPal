2. Tour Bookings
Payment Terms
1
A 100 RMB deposit per tour (per group) is required to secure your reservation.
2
The remaining balance is payable at the end of your tour via Alipay.
3
Additional services (e.g., private car, cruises, attraction tickets) require full prepayment.
Cancellation & Refunds
Scenario
Outcome

3. Tickets & Partner Services
Payment Terms
1
Full payment is required upon booking for all tickets and third-party services (e.g., attractions, shows, cruises).
Cancellation & Refunds
1
Refunds are subject to the policies of our partner providers.
2
We will clearly communicate all applicable terms prior to confirmation.
3
In cases of weather disruptions or partner-related cancellations, we will assist in securing a full or partial refund, depending on the provider's policy.
