Guide Arrangement for Wulong
When visiting Wulong, you typically cover 2 scenic spots in a day. To make this possible, your guide will brief you on the history, nature, and background of each spot at the entrance, then drop you off while you explore. If your guide accompanies you inside, the group must park at the tourism center, take the shuttle bus in, and return the same way — which limits the day to just 1 scenic spot.
