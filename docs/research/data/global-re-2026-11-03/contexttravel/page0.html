          Explore More Than 80 Cities with Context

          From London to Lisbon, Paris to Prague, Context tours offer you a way to uncover and really understand a city.

          London

          Winding river bends and bridges chart a path past London’s landmarks, narrating centuries of commerce, creativity, and culture.

          Rome

          Walk streets where emperors, popes, and republics left stone and story in every piazza, shaping Rome’s timeless civic life.

          Tokyo

          Hidden alleys, temples, and neon-lit streets reveal Tokyo’s rhythm where centuries of tradition meet relentless innovation.

          Paris

          Boulevards pulse with cafés, art, and architecture where museums, galleries, and theaters define Paris’ global cultural influence.

          Madrid

          Boulevards pulse with royal palaces, vibrant plazas, and art institutions anchoring Madrid’s central role in Spanish life.

          Oxford

          Quadrangles and spires rise above cobblestone lanes where Oxford’s centuries of scholarship and literary life still thrive.

          Milan

          Futuristic meets Gothic in Milan, where fashion houses, art galleries, food and architecture converge in Italy’s design hub.

          Mexico City

          Terraced neighborhoods hold echoes of Aztec temples and colonial plazas, shaping Mexico City’s vibrant, layered urban energy.

          Amsterdam

          Canals guide the eye past gabled houses, bustling markets, and cultural hubs in Amsterdam, shaped by trade and tolerance.

          San Sebastián

          La Concha’s crescent bay frames old town pintxo bars and festivals that define San Sebastián’s coastal culinary culture.

          Marrakech

          Ancient splendor meets desert glamour in Marrakech, where vibrant riads, souks and palm gardens color Morocco’s timeless allure.

          Seville

          Orange-lined plazas invite you into Seville’s streets alive with flamenco, Moorish architecture, and Andalusian culture.

          Barcelona

          Stone boulevards pulse with Gaudí’s curves and Catalan traditions in Barcelona’s streets, a city alive with creativity and design.

          Florence

          Renaissance patronage shaped Florence, where Michelangelo, Dante, and the Medicis left a legacy of art, culture, and ambition.

          Venice

          Canals usher you past palazzos and secret calli in Venice, where waterborne life and artistic legacies define every turn.

          Kyoto

          Temples, gardens and shrines come together to reflect Kyoto's spirit, where centuries of ritual shape Japan’s cultural core.

          Athens

          Ancient agora paths walked by Athenian thinkers of democracy and philosophy which still shape Western ideas of civilization today.

          New York City

          Boroughs blend into a single energy in New York City, where immigrant waves fuel finance, arts, media, and cultural innovation.

          Prague

          Prague's river bridges punctuate the city, whose thousand‑year architecture rallies Gothic, Baroque, and storytelling life.

          Lisbon

          Hills frame Lisbon’s colorful quarters where Phoenician roots and Age of Discovery legacies mingle with modern city energy.

          Bologna

          Historic Italian city with medieval towers, long porticoed streets, rich food culture, and Europe’s oldest university.

          Berlin

          Berlin's districts bring together wall fragments, wartime pasts, and architectural reinvention in this city of civic ambition.

          Naples

          Alleyways open to lively piazzas where opera, Neapolitan song, and globally loved pizza reflect Naples’ spirited cultural energy.

          Edinburgh

          Power built Edinburgh’s castle and courts, while its Old Town spine and festivals keep scholarly and creative energy alive.

          Cape Town

          Table Mountain, coastal paths, and historic ports proudly frame Cape Town’s rich mix of culture, commerce, and natural drama.

          Verona

          Historic northern Italian city with Roman ruins, winding medieval streets, the Arena amphitheater, and layered heritage.

          Munich

          Streets wind through Munich’s squares and beer gardens where art, music, and industrial history converge into vibrant city life.

          Vienna

          Impressive imperial avenues, coffeehouse corners, and famed composers echo from Habsburg's past through to city life today.

          Salzburg

          Gardens and baroque churches rise behind fortress walls in a city born of ecclesiastical power and Mozart’s enduring music.

          Oslo

          Fjord views meet modern architecture in Oslo, where museums, royal history, and Nordic design inform the city's life and culture.

          Santorini

          Cliffs rise from a vast volcanic coastline where white‑washed villages and sea views shape Santorini’s timeless island character.

          Pisa

          Leaning monuments draw eyes upward in a compact city where medieval ambition and architectural quirk meet on the Piazza.

          Fes

          Ancient mystique meets living tradition in Fes, where bustling souks, carved madrasas and vivid tanneries shape the city’s soul.

          Seoul

          Seoul’s layered cityscape unites ancient palaces, markets and modern towers, reflecting the dynamic history and creative momentum.

          Singapore

          Singapore seamlessly blends Malay, Chinese, Indian, and colonial heritage into a future-focused, forward-looking, cultural city.

          Porto

          Hillsides beside the Douro cradle centuries of markets, famed port wine, and stone façades that reveal Porto’s mercantile spirit.

          Glasgow

          Glasgow’s storied streets, where shipyards morph into music venues and murals, map a city reborn from industry into creativity.

          Sydney

          Harbor, coastline, and neighborhood life define Sydney, where history and contemporary culture meet dramatic natural beauty.

          Belfast

          Shipyards, waterfront renewal, and contested history shape a city defined by memory and reinvention along the River Lagan.

          Krakow

          Krakow’s medieval guild halls and Jewish quarter echo a rich tapestry of Poland’s scholarly, cultural, and historic heritage.

          Normandy

          Rolling coastlines and medieval towns echo Normandy’s Viking, medieval, and WWII histories, shaping a rugged cultural identity.

          Nice

          Promenades line the Mediterranean in Nice, where historic old quarters meet French and Italian influences along sunlit shores.

          Siena

          Siena’s winding brick lanes, Gothic palaces, and bustling piazzas reveal a city steeped in rich medieval character and history.

          Sicily

          Sicily’s rugged landscapes, baroque towns, and ancient ruins reveal a Mediterranean island shaped by millennia of migration.

          Reykjavik

          Reykjavík’s harbor, streets, and volcanic landscapes bring sagas, art, and Nordic design into focus as part of modern city life.
