Chongqing Evening Food Tour

Daily at 6pm, Duration: 3.5 hours

$59 USD / person

This walking tour covers approx 2.5km / 1.5mi

All food & drinks included

The Essentials:

Known as China’s Mountain City, Chongqing developed a food culture as bold as its landscape. Inspired by decades of dock-workers passing through, the cuisine here features highlights like Chongqing’s famous hotpot, and noodles topped with chili sauce and stewed peas. This walking food tour introduces local restaurants and dishes that have shaped everyday life in Chongqing for generations.

Inclusions

Far more than a meal’s worth of Chongqing food, local beer, and soft drinks at 3 local restaurants, plus a traditional dessert and iconic city views.

Why Join?

This is the perfect way to experience Chongqing beyond the famous skyline (although you’ll see that too!). Walk through residential neighborhoods and visit family-run restaurants that define the city’s culinary culture.

Group Size

Small groups of 2-12 people per tour.

Start & Stop Locations

This tour starts near Linjiangmen Subway Station in central Chongqing and ends near Hongyadong’s incredible night view. An email will be sent with detailed meeting location information immediately after booking.

Cancellation Policy

Reservations cancelled 24 hours or more before your tour start time are fully refundable.

What You'll Do:

Meeting Location:

This tour starts near Linjiangmen Subway Station. An email with detailed meeting instructions will be sent immediately after booking.

Kuixing Building & China's "8D City"

Stand on what looks like the ground floor…then look over the edge to discover you’re actually 22 stories above another street. It’s one of the best places to understand why Chongqing’s layered cityscape is unlike anywhere else in China.

Pea & Minced Pork Noodles

Meet Mr. Gong, who has spent more than a decade perfecting Chongqing’s favorite comfort food: Chongqing xiaomian. Handmade noodles, slow-cooked yellow peas, minced pork, silky tofu pudding, and chili oil come together in a bowl that perfectly captures Chongqing’s balance of bold flavors and comforting textures.

Eat Chongqing's Everyday Classics

Long before Chongqing became famous for hotpot, locals gathered around tables filled with small shared dishes served with rice or tofu. Enjoy favorites like melt-in-your-mouth steamed pork belly, braised beef, and seasonal vegetables.

Walk Through China's Mountain City

Wander through the historic neighborhoods around Jiefangbei and Daijia Alley, where old stone staircases and hidden lanes sit beneath Chongqing’s modern skyline. Once a major river port and wartime capital, this area has seen merchants, journalists, laborers, and families pass through for generations.

Authentic Neighborhood Hotpot​

Born along the Yangtze River over 100 years ago, Chongqing hotpot started as a simple meal for dock-workers and boat crew. Today, it’s a city obsession with more than 30,000 hot pot restaurants. Try the flavors that made it famous: rich beef tallow broth, fiery chilies, and numbing Sichuan peppercorns at a local neighborhood restaurant.

Hongya Cave by Night

Take in the glowing view of Hongyadong, where traditional architecture meets Chongqing’s modern skyline. This is one of the most famous night-views in the city!

A Sweet Chongqing Treat

After an evening of bold flavors, end on a sweet note with a traditional handmade dessert, just steps from Hongya Cave and overlooking Chongqing’s iconic Qiansimen Bridge.

Plus More Surprises!

* This itinerary is subject to changes and adjustments based on daily vendor operations and availability, but it will always be awesome.

With more than 30 million residents, Chongqing is one of the world's largest cities.

For centuries, everything from tea and salt to people and ideas traveled through Chongqing’s rivers, helping shape one of China’s most important trading ports before roads and railways even existed.

Its dramatic landscape has shaped everything here, from the architecture to the food. Locals joke that Chongqing is an “8D city,” where buildings have entrances on different floors, streets cross above rooftops, and getting lost is part of the experience.

This tour goes beyond the famous skyline and hotpot to explore the neighborhoods where people actually live and eat. Along the way you’ll discover the comfort foods, hidden history, and family-run eateries that have kept Chongqing’s food culture alive for generations.

Frequently Asked Questions:

How much food and drink will I get?

This is not a tasting tour, you should come hungry and you will get more than a full meal of food – usually guests cannot finish everything and you’ll want to make sure you pace yourself. We also provide local beer, soft drinks, and bottled water throughout the tour.

What if I’m vegetarian, gluten-free, have allergies, or have special dietary requirements?

All dietary requests must be communicated at the time of booking. When booking online, you will have the opportunity to let us know if you have any dietary requirements or special requests. Same day requests cannot be guaranteed. Please use the following information to understand if this tour is right for you.

Vegetarians & Pescatarians: We’ll make sure you have something to eat at every stop, but you won’t be able to eat everything and you may miss the highlight dish at a couple stops. This is because we visit small restaurants that specialize in one dish, and they don’t always offer substitutions. This tour will be extremely limited for vegans.

Spicy Tolerance: Chongqing is known for it’s use of chili in every dish. While spicy level can be slightly adjusted, it is impossible to avoid spice entirely.

Gluten Free: Wheat products are used at every stop in sauces like soy sauce and in tour highlights like noodles. There are no substitutes or alternatives available. 

This tour is not recommended for anyone with severe allergies to common foods as we cannot guarantee the absence of those foods or that cross-contamination won’t occur.

Will I need to walk very far?

This is a walking tour. This tour covers about 1.5 miles / 2.5 km by foot, so we recommend leaving the heels at home. This distance is split up between 4 restaurants plus stops for sightseeing and photo taking, so it’s roughly a 5-10 minute walk between each stop and a great chance to digest a bit. 

Please note: The tour route does include stairs, inclined streets, and uneven surfaces. It is not suitable for those requiring mobility assistance.

Where does the tour start and end?

This tour starts at 6pm at Linjiangmen Subway Station in central Chongqing. You’ll get the exact location and map pins in your confirmation email immediately after booking. 

The tour ends at our dessert stop near Hongyadong’s incredible night view. There are plenty of options for you to continue exploring the area on your own, or your guide can assist you with directions for getting back to your hotel.

How many people will be on my tour?

Our minimum is 2 and maximum is 12. There’s no flag to follow, or megaphone, or tacky uniforms. It’s small enough to be intimate, but big enough to get away with trying several dishes at each stop. Bottom line is, you’ll follow your expert guide through a maze of streets, alleys, and buildings for local experience and make friends along the way.

Private tours can be arranged, by request, for an additional fee.

What if it's raining or there is inclement weather?

All of our tours are rain-or-shine, so we suggest you dress for the weather. If rain is possible, an umbrella, along with shoes that can get wet, is a good idea. In rare cases of extreme weather, your guide will cancel the tour and provide a full refund.

Can I book a private tour for myself or my group/event?

Yes, this food tour can be booked privately, either for yourself or for a group or special occasion. We’ve hosted everything from birthdays and bachelor/ette parties to corporate events and school groups. Just let us know the date, time and group size, and we’ll handle the rest – including the itinerary, dietary restrictions, logistics, and even extras like birthday cakes. We can accommodate groups of any size, splitting large parties into smaller groups if needed. Typically, private tours with 8 or more people won’t incur an extra private tour fee. Contact us directly to get started!

Do you offer discounts?

Yes! You’ll get a promo code in your confirmation email that will give you a 10% discount on all future Lost Plate tours (discounts are applied automatically if booking more than one tour at the same time on our website). It doesn’t matter if the tour is in the same city or a different city, we want to keep feeding you as long as you let us. 

What is your cancellation policy?

Bookings canceled 24 hours or more before your tour are fully refundable (100% refund). Bookings canceled within 24 hours notice are not refundable. If you would like to reschedule your tour with less than 24 hours notice, we will need to charge 50% of your total since we are unable to fill your spots at short notice.
