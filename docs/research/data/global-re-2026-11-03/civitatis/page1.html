Top Tours and Activities in Chongqing | Book Online

Discover Chongqing, a bustling metropolis in southwest China where dramatic landscapes merge with modern city life. Known for its hilly streets, whispering rivers, and spicy cuisine, this vivid city offers a sensory adventure at every turn. Whether you're wandering through vibrant markets or gazing at the skyline along the Yangtze River, it's a destination that excites curious travelers.

A Glimpse Into the Past

Once a key stronghold during World War II, today the city is a vital economic and cultural hub. Its rich heritage is woven into ancient temples, old town quarters, and the stories of boats navigating busy waterways for centuries. Layer upon layer of history awaits exploration.

When To Go

Spring (March to May) and autumn (September to November) are the best times to visit. Mild temperatures and less humidity mean more comfortable days for sightseeing, taking river cruises, or sampling hotpot on an outdoor terrace.

What is Chongqing famous for?

This city is most famous for its fiery hotpot, stunning nightscapes, the Three Gorges, and as the starting point for river cruises on the Yangtze. Don’t miss the spicy cuisine or the vibrant nightlife along riverbanks.

How do I get around?

Urban rail, ferries, and buses make city travel easy. For short distances, taxis or ride apps are convenient too. Many attractions are close together, perfect for exploring on foot.
