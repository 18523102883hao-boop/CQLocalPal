Food route

Chongqing food tour: hotpot, noodles and local tables

Chongqing food is intense, social and deeply local. We make it accessible, safe and fun for visitors who do not read Chinese menus.

DurationEvening or half-day, flexible
PaceRelaxed and flexible
Best seasonYear-round
Best forFood lovers
GroupPrivate — just your party
Guide languagesEnglish-speaking guide included; Spanish, Korean or Russian on request, subject to availability
PricingQuote on request

What to expect

A local guide walks you through Chongqing's real tables, so you eat where locals eat without wrestling with Chinese menus.

Orientation

Meet, and set your spice and comfort level

We meet your guide, talk through what you like, flag any allergies, and agree how spicy you want to go. Non-spicy tastes are always kept on the table.

Street snacks & market

Xiaomian noodles, snacks and a market walk

Taste Chongqing xiaomian noodles, ice jelly and riverside snacks, and wander a working food street where your guide explains what you are eating.

Hotpot table

Sit down to a proper Chongqing hotpot

Settle in at a trusted hotpot table. Your guide handles ordering, sets up a mild split-pot if you prefer, and shows you how locals dip and eat.

Who this guide is for

Food travellers looking for hotpot, street snacks and local eating experiences in Chongqing.

Planning notes

  • Signature tastes: hotpot, xiaomian noodles, grilled fish, ice jelly, tea houses and late-night riverside snacks.
  • Comfort controls: non-spicy options, hygiene-aware choices, dietary notes and private transport after dinner.
  • Best format: evening food walk plus hotpot, or full-day food route woven into sightseeing.

What's included

Included
  • Private English-speaking food guide for your party only
  • Private vehicle and driver where the route needs it, with a safe ride after dinner
  • Curated tastings across hotpot, xiaomian noodles and street snacks, all arranged for you
  • Ordering and translation handled, with dietary needs and spice level adjusted
  • Local recommendations and hygiene-aware choices along the way
Not included
  • International and domestic flights or rail tickets
  • Hotels, unless accommodation is stated in your package
  • The food and drink bill itself, and any personal spending
  • Tips, travel insurance and visa costs
