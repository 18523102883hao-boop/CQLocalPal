Comfort travel

Senior-friendly Chongqing tour planning

Chongqing is vertical, so comfort planning matters. We build routes around elevators, vehicle access, shade, rest stops and walking level.

DurationFlexible, half or full day
PaceLight, relaxed and flexible
Best seasonSpring and autumn most comfortable

How a comfortable day flows

Chongqing is vertical, so we plan a comfortable, low-strain route with rest stops built in.

Relaxed start

Door-to-door pickup

We start when you are ready, with no early rush. The private vehicle collects you at your hotel door and drops you close to each sight.

Gentle sightseeing

Lift and escalator routing

We favour viewpoints reached by lift or escalator over long stair climbs, with shade and benches along the way. Walking is kept short and optional.

Rest and refuel

Unhurried meal and rest

A calm, non-spicy meal at a comfortable restaurant, with time to sit and rest. We adjust the afternoon to how you feel.

Easy return

Back whenever you like

If you tire early, we return to the hotel early — the private car means the day bends around you, not a fixed group schedule.

Who this guide is for

Senior travellers and families planning a comfortable private Chongqing itinerary.

Planning notes

  • Comfort controls: private vehicle, shorter walks, hotel location, elevator-aware attractions, flexible start times and non-spicy meals.
  • Recommended route: city icons over 3 days or Dazu heritage with a slower pace. Wulong works best as an overnight trip.
  • What to tell us: mobility level, stairs tolerance, preferred meal style and whether you want early starts or relaxed mornings.

What's included

Included
  • Private English-speaking guide who paces the day to your comfort
  • Private vehicle with door-to-door transfers and drop-offs close to each sight
  • A comfortable, low-strain route planned around lifts, escalators and rest stops
  • Core attraction planning and tickets for the sights on your route
  • Non-spicy meal arrangement and dietary coordination on request
Not included
  • International and domestic flights or long-distance rail
  • Hotels, unless your package states accommodation is included
  • Personal expenses, gratuities and any wheelchair or mobility-aid rental
  • Travel insurance and China visa
