What to include in a trade request

A precise brief helps us protect your response time and quote on the same basis as your sales team.

Traveller profile

FIT or group, ages, mobility, dietary needs and preferred guide language.

Trip logistics

Dates, arrival and departure points, hotel level and onward route.

Commercial model

Tell us whether you need net rates, commissionable pricing, white-label fulfilment or a co-branded proposal.

Service scope

Guides, vehicles, tickets, meals, accommodation and emergency contact expectations.

Clear boundaries before confirmation

Rates, payment schedule, cancellation terms, supplier availability and responsibilities are confirmed in writing for each partner or booking. We do not publish a generic trade rate because dates, group size and service scope materially change local costs.
