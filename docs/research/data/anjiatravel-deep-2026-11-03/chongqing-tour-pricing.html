What a day of private service includes

The question people usually ask is “how much is a car for a day in Chongqing?” or “how much for an English-speaking guide?” For sightseeing days we don’t sell those separately, for a practical reason: a driver doesn’t narrate, and a guide doesn’t drive. Book them apart and you spend the day coordinating between two people, with nobody owning the pace of the day. The one exception is a simple one-way transfer: an airport pickup, a station transfer or a run to the cruise pier can be booked on its own.

Our day is one unit: one licensed English-speaking guide, one vehicle sized to your group (a seven-seater for up to four guests) and one driver, with the vehicle yours until eleven at night. Hongya Cave’s lights don’t come on until dark, and the traffic at Liziba is best at dusk — a Chongqing day runs into the night by nature, and an itinerary whose car clocks off early cuts out the hours most worth seeing.

Entrance tickets, meals, and the guide’s and driver’s meal allowance are not included. We book those at the actual price with no mark-up, and every item is written into your quote before you book.

What determines your price

A private trip is priced on what it actually includes, so your quote reflects your choices rather than a fixed package. These are the main things that move the number up or down.

Hotel level and standard

The class of hotel you choose is usually the single biggest factor. A comfortable well-located four-star and a landmark luxury property sit at very different levels, and you decide which suits you.

Group size

The private guide and vehicle are shared by your whole party, so the cost per person usually falls as the group grows. Couples, families and small groups of friends each work out differently.

Trip length and number of days

More days mean more nights, meals, guiding and transport, so the total naturally scales with length. A city stopover and a multi-day journey are quoted very differently.

Season and dates

Peak holiday periods push up hotel and transport costs across the whole region, while quieter dates are gentler. Telling us your dates lets us quote accurately and suggest better-value windows.

Route, attractions and add-ons

Where you go shapes the cost: staying in the city is different from adding Wulong Karst, Dazu Rock Carvings, a river cruise or a linked trip on to Zhangjiajie. Extra tickets and experiences are added transparently.

Guide, vehicle and special needs

An English-speaking private guide is standard. We can also arrange a Mandarin-speaking guide, and Spanish, Korean or Russian guides on request, subject to availability. Things like a larger vehicle or step-free accessibility are built into the quote where needed.

Why a package costs less than booking separately

It can feel like doing it all yourself must be cheaper. In practice, a well-planned private package usually costs less than booking each part on your own, and it removes the stress.

Local wholesale rates

As a licensed local operator we book hotels, tickets and transport at trade rates you cannot easily get as an individual traveller, and pass that footing on to you.

One team, no double margins

One team coordinates your whole trip, so you are not paying a chain of separate agents and middlemen each adding their own mark-up on top.

No forced-shopping detours

We never build in forced-shopping stops. Your paid time on the ground goes to the places you came to see, not to detours that waste the days you paid for.

Costly mistakes avoided

A local team plans the connections, timings and bookings that are easy to get wrong from abroad, so you avoid missed transfers, sold-out tickets and gaps that quietly cost more than a package would.

Two service levels

Comfort private

Everything fully private and well looked after, with comfortable, well-located four-star hotels, a private English-speaking guide and a private car with driver throughout. The relaxed way to see Chongqing properly without stretching the budget.

Luxury private

The same fully private care raised to landmark five-star and luxury hotels, senior guides, premium vehicles and thoughtful extras. For travellers who want the finest stays and a more polished, slower-paced experience.
