Wulong Private Day Trip from Chongqing: Hotel Pickup, Route and 2026 Price

A licensed Chongqing agency's private day trip to the Wulong Karst: picked up at your hotel at 7:00, the Three Natural Bridges and Longshui Gorge with an English-speaking guide, back in the city for the night lights. One price per group. Prefer a slower pace? The two-day version is below.

The Wulong day trip in short

We are a licensed local travel agency in Chongqing (licence L-CQ-100787) and we run Wulong as a private day trip: at 7:00 we pick you up at your hotel in the main city, you see the Three Natural Bridges and Longshui Gorge, and you are back at Jiefangbei at about 20:00. For 1–4 guests the 2026 price is ¥2,600 per group, with a 7-seat MPV, driver and English-speaking guide; entrance tickets are booked at the price posted on the day and paid separately. Send us your date and group size on WhatsApp and we reply with a written itinerary and total.

How the day runs

TimeWhat happens
7:00Pickup at your hotel in the main city of Chongqing

From September to April the scenic areas are open 8:30–16:30 (later from May to August), which is why the day starts at 7:00 and runs about 13 hours. If you would rather not do it in one day, stay a night and see Fairy Mountain the next morning: see the two-day version below.

Rather not do it in one day? The two-day version

An overnight near Wulong adds Furong Cave, the Fairy Mountain meadows and a gentler pace. This is how the two days run.

Day 1

Chongqing to Wulong and the Three Natural Bridges

We drive from Chongqing to Wulong and head to the Three Natural Bridges, a valley of giant stone arches used as a film location. A lift takes you down into the karst valley, and your guide keeps the shuttle and timed walking routes running smoothly.

Day 1

Optional Furong Cave, then return or overnight

If time and energy allow, we add Furong Cave, a large show cave nearby. As a day trip we then drive back to Chongqing; if you would rather slow down, we stop overnight near Wulong to keep the day comfortable.

Day 2

Fairy Mountain highlands, then back to Chongqing

On the overnight option we spend a relaxed morning at Fairy Mountain, a highland meadow area that stays cooler in summer and suits slower photography. We then drive back to Chongqing at an easy pace.

Who this guide is for

Travellers searching for Wulong, Three Natural Bridges or nature day trips from Chongqing.

  • Core route: Chongqing to Wulong, Three Natural Bridges, optional Furong Cave or Fairy Mountain, return or overnight.
