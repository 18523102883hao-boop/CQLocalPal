Our guides

Who stands behind these guides

Our Chongqing guides are written by the Anjia team and reviewed by Jiang Pingchuan, our founder and legal representative, who is responsible for what they say. Anjia was registered in Chongqing in September 2023 and is a licensed inbound travel agency (licence L-CQ-100787).

Before we write about a place, we go there ourselves or ask the guide who was there that day. For things that change, like ticket prices, opening hours and transport, we quote only the official government or attraction source and show the month we checked it. If we can't find an official source, we say “we confirm this for you on the day” and don't print a number.

Jiang Pingchuan's team also runs every trip on the ground: the car, the guide, tickets and anything that goes wrong. If you spot a mistake on any page, tell us on WhatsApp. We check it, fix it the same day and update the date on that page.
