How a family day flows

The 8D city is exciting for families when the day is paced well, with shorter walks and food everyone can enjoy.

Morning

Icon sights at an easy pace

Start with crowd-pleasing highlights such as the Liziba train-through-building, a viewpoint or a short river-cruise moment, with the private car handling the vertical distances.

Midday

A meal everyone enjoys

Hotpot with a mild or split-pot broth, or an easy local restaurant, so both adventurous eaters and cautious ones are happy. We coordinate spice levels in advance.

Afternoon

Flexible choice by interest

Choose by the group's interests and energy — an old street, a museum or science-style stop by request, or a slower afternoon. The private schedule bends around naps, breaks and weather.

Evening

Neon night view, then back

A short taste of the famous neon skyline, timed so tired members can head back early while others stay a little longer.

Who this guide is for

Families planning a Chongqing visit with children or multi-generation travellers.

Planning notes

  • Family-friendly picks: Liziba, river cruise, panda or science-style stops by request, hotpot with mild broth, short viewpoint walks.
  • Private advantage: naps, snack breaks and weather changes are easier when the route is not fixed to a group schedule.
  • Best length: 3 days for city highlights, 5 days if adding Wulong with a slower pace.

What's included

Included
  • Private English-speaking guide experienced with mixed-age family groups
  • Private vehicle and driver with flexible timing and hotel pickup
  • Core attraction planning and tickets tailored to your family's interests
  • Meal coordination with spice levels adjusted for the whole group
  • Flexible pacing with room for naps, breaks and weather changes
Not included
  • International and domestic flights or long-distance rail
  • Hotels, unless your package states accommodation is included
  • Personal expenses, snacks and gratuities
  • Travel insurance and China visa
