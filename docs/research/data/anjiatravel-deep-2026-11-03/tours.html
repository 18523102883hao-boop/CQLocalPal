3 days 2 nights · flagship

Chongqing Panorama — the Cyberpunk City

The complete 8D city in a long weekend: neon nights, the train through a building, the original hotpot, an imperial-style palace banquet and giant pandas — all privately guided.

FROM
$380
per person
Best forFirst visit / couples / photographers PaceMedium, strongest at night Walking2-4 hours daily, adjustable Hotel styleCentral 4★ by Jiefangbei (upgradable)
DAY01

Arrival & first neon

Private airport or rail pickup and check-in by Jiefangbei. Tonight: authentic Chongqing hotpot, a walk through the city's futuristic core, then Hongya Cave as it ignites in gold — and on Saturdays (schedule permitting) the Nanbin riverside drone show.

DAY02

8D city, decoded

Glide over the Yangtze on the river cableway, wander Longmenhao Old Street and the stepped lanes of Shibati, then sit down to the Baguo Palace Banquet — an imperial-style SVIP lunch. Afternoon: the Liziba light-rail slicing through an apartment block, the Testbed 2 creative park and Ciqikou ancient town. Evening options: live-music dining on Nanbin Road or the Guanyinqiao bar street.

DAY03

Pandas & farewell

Morning with the giant pandas at Chongqing Zoo and a last graze along the Guanyinqiao food street. Private transfer to your airport or station, timed to your flight — or roll straight into a longer journey below.

4 days 3 nights · city + heritage

Chongqing & Dazu

The cyberpunk city plus the serene UNESCO masterpiece of the Dazu Rock Carvings — a thousand years of cliff-face sculpture, an hour and a half from the neon.

FROM
$580
per person
Best forCulture / seniors / slower day PaceGentle to medium WalkingMuseum and grotto paths Hotel styleCentral 4★ by Jiefangbei (upgradable)
DAY01

Arrival & neon night

Private pickup and check-in by Jiefangbei. Tonight: authentic Chongqing hotpot, then Hongya Cave after dark — with the Nanbin riverside drone show on Saturdays, schedule permitting.

DAY02

The 8D city

Yangtze cableway, Longmenhao Old Street and the stepped lanes of Shibati, the Baguo Palace Banquet SVIP lunch, then Liziba train-through-building, Testbed 2 creative park and Ciqikou ancient town. Evening: Nanbin Road live-music dining or the Guanyinqiao bar street, as you like.

DAY03

Dazu Rock Carvings

Day trip to the UNESCO-listed Baoding and Beishan grottoes — thousands of vivid Buddhist, Taoist and Confucian figures carved into the cliffs from the 9th–13th centuries.

DAY04

Pandas & departure

Morning with the giant pandas at Chongqing Zoo and a stroll through the Guanyinqiao food street, then your private transfer onward.

5 days 4 nights · city + karst + Wujiang

Cyberpunk, Wulong Karst & the Wujiang Gallery

Pair the neon megacity with the otherworldly UNESCO karst of Wulong, the high grasslands of Fairy Mountain, the Miao citadel of Jiuli City and a cruise through the Wujiang river gallery.

FROM
$680
per person
Best forCity + nature + living culture PaceMedium, two out-of-town days WalkingSteps and park paths Hotel styleCity 4★ + one Wulong mountain night
DAY01

Arrival & free neon evening

Private pickup and check-in by Jiefangbei. The evening is yours — we point you to the neon core and the Bayi Road food street, minutes from your hotel.

DAY02

Into Wulong

Scenic drive to the Wulong Karst. Three Natural Bridges: colossal limestone arches and the canyon film-set of Transformers: Age of Extinction and Zhang Yimou's Curse of the Golden Flower, then the cool meadows and alpine air of Fairy Mountain grassland. Overnight in Wulong.

DAY03

Jiuli City & the Wujiang Gallery

Morning at Chiyou Jiuli City in Pengshui — a vast citadel of stacked Miao pavilions — then a boat cruise through the emerald gorge of the Wujiang Gallery. Return to Chongqing by evening.

DAY04

The 8D city in one day

Yangtze river cableway, Longmenhao Old Street, the stepped lanes of Shibati, the Liziba train-through-building, Testbed 2 creative park and Ciqikou ancient town — finishing at Hongya Cave as the neon ignites.

DAY05

Sleep in & departure

A slow morning, then your private transfer to the airport or station, timed to your flight.
