What luxury changes

Luxury in Chongqing is not only hotel category. It is better routing, fewer friction points, stronger dinner reservations and a guide who knows when to slow down.

DAY01

VIP arrival and skyline base

Private pickup, hotel check-in support, tailored first evening and reserved dinner.

DAY02

Curated 8D city

Hongya Cave, Liziba, private viewpoints, tea or gallery stops, with vehicle repositioning to reduce walking stress.

DAY03

Premium extension

Add Wulong, Dazu or Zhangjiajie with upgraded hotels, better timing and slower private pacing.
