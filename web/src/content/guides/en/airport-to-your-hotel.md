Jiangbei Airport (CKG) sits about 20–25 km north of Jiefangbei, the downtown most visitors stay in. Four ways cover that distance; two of them are how we'd send our own family, one is fine in a pinch, and one you should refuse every time.

## Option 1 — Metro (best value, surprisingly easy)

**Line 10** runs from Terminal 3 directly toward the city; transfer at Hongtudi to reach Jiefangbei, Guanyinqiao and the main areas. Trains run roughly every 6–10 minutes from early morning to around 23:00.

- **Cost:** about ¥2–7
- **Time:** 50–70 minutes door to door
- **When to choose:** daytime arrivals, light luggage, or if your hotel sits near a Line 1/6/10 station
- **Tip:** use Alipay's transport code — no ticket machine, no card, no fuss

## Option 2 — Official taxi from the rank (best door-to-door)

Follow the signed taxi queues outside the terminals. Taxis are metered and honest; the rank is marshalled and there's an official receipt printer in every cab.

- **Cost:** ¥60–100 to Jiefangbei depending on traffic, plus ¥10 tolls
- **Time:** 30–50 minutes
- **When to choose:** after 23:00, with luggage or kids, or if your hotel isn't near a metro stop
- **Rule:** only the official queue. Anyone who approaches you inside the arrivals hall offering a "cheap taxi" is a scam — wave them off and keep walking

## Option 3 — Didi (best if you have the app working)

Didi's English interface works well in Chongqing, pickup points are signed at the terminals, and payment happens in-app.

- **Cost:** similar to a taxi, sometimes a touch less with coupons
- **When to choose:** if Alipay/Didi are already set up (see our connectivity guide), or your hotel sent you a pinned location
- **Catch:** needs data and a working payment method — which is why we tell guests to sort both before flying

## Option 4 — Airport bus (only if the metro has stopped)

Night buses cover the period after the metro closes, dropping at central stops. Fine after a late landing, slower and less obvious with luggage. If you land after midnight, honestly, take the official taxi instead.

## The two scams that still exist

1. **"Taxi" solicitors inside the hall** — unlicensed cars with inflated flat fares. Refuse. The real rank is outside, marshalled, metered.
2. **"Your hotel is closed, I take you to another one"** — a classic worldwide; your hotel is not closed. Insist on your address, or show the driver the Chinese address screenshot we tell every guest to carry.
